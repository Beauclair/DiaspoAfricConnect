Account & Data Deletion

How to Delete Your Account

You can delete your account and all associated data directly from the app:

  1. Open DiaspoAfricConnect and sign in to your account.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Scroll down and tap "Delete Account".
  4. Confirm the deletion when prompted.

What Data Is Deleted

When you delete your account, the following data is permanently removed:

  • Your user profile (name, email, country of residence, languages)
  • All business listings you created
  • All lawyer profiles you registered
  • All reviews you submitted
  • All verification requests you submitted
  • Your Firebase Authentication account
⚠️ This action is permanent and cannot be undone. Once your account is deleted, all your data is permanently erased and cannot be recovered.

Can't Access the App?

If you're unable to delete your account through the app, you can request deletion by emailing us:

Email: infos@weskillupcenter.com
Subject: Account Deletion Request
Include: The email address associated with your account

We will process your request and delete all associated data within 30 days.

For more information about how we handle your data, please see our Privacy Policy.
