Data Deletion

We respect your right to control your personal data. You can delete your account and all associated data at any time using either method below.

Option 1 — Delete from the App (Recommended)

Steps

  1. 1 Open DiaspoAfricConnect and log in.
  2. 2 Go to the Profile tab.
  3. 3 Scroll down and tap "Delete Account".
  4. 4 Confirm the deletion in the dialog.

⚠️ This action is permanent and cannot be undone. Once your account is deleted, all your data is permanently erased and cannot be recovered.

Option 2 — Request Deletion by Email

If you cannot access your account, send an email to:

📧 infos@weskillupcenter.com

Include the email address associated with your account. We will verify your identity and process the deletion within 7 business days.

What Gets Deleted

When your account is deleted, the following data is permanently removed:

  • Your user profile (name, email, photo, preferences)
  • All business listings you created
  • All attorney profiles you registered
  • All verification requests you submitted
  • All images you uploaded (business photos and attorney profile photos)
  • Your reviews are anonymised (author changed to "Deleted User")

Data in third-party services (Sentry crash logs, PostHog analytics) is automatically purged according to their retention policies, typically within 90 days.
