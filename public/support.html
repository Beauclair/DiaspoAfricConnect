Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create an account?

Open the app, tap "Sign Up", and register with your email address or Google account. It's free!

How do I list my business?

Once signed in, go to your Profile tab and tap "My Businesses" → "Add Business". Fill in your business details, add photos, and submit.

How do I list my attorney profile?

Go to your Profile tab and tap "My Attorney Profiles" → "Add Attorney Profile". Complete the form with your qualifications, specializations, and contact details.

How do I leave a review?

Open any business or attorney profile and scroll to the Reviews section. Tap "Write a Review", give a star rating, and share your experience.

How do I save a business?

Tap the bookmark icon on any business listing to save it. Access your saved businesses from your Profile tab.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Profile → scroll down → tap "Delete Account". This permanently removes your account and all associated data. You can also request deletion via our Data Deletion page.

Is the app free?

Yes! DiaspoAfricConnect is completely free to use — browsing, listing your business, and leaving reviews are all free.
