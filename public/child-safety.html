DiaspoAfricConnect

Child Safety Standards

Effective date: October 9, 2026
Last updated: October 9, 2026

Our commitment: DiaspoAfricConnect is committed to providing a safe environment for all users and has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) content on our platform.

1. Platform Overview

DiaspoAfricConnect is a community platform designed exclusively for adults (18+) that helps the African diaspora discover local businesses, find attorneys, and access legal guides in their host country.

The app is not directed at children. Our target audience is adults aged 18 and older, and we do not knowingly allow minors to create accounts or use our services.

2. Standards Against CSAE

DiaspoAfricConnect maintains the following standards to prevent child sexual abuse and exploitation on our platform:

  • Zero tolerance policy: Any content depicting, promoting, or facilitating child sexual abuse or exploitation is strictly prohibited and will be immediately removed.
  • Account termination: Users who upload, share, or distribute CSAE material will have their accounts permanently terminated without notice.
  • Law enforcement reporting: We will report any identified CSAE material to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) via the CyberTipline, and cooperate fully with law enforcement investigations.
  • Content removal: Any CSAE content identified on our platform will be removed immediately upon discovery or report.

3. Age Restrictions

  • DiaspoAfricConnect is restricted to users aged 18 and older.
  • We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction).
  • If we discover that a user is under 18, their account will be suspended and their data deleted.
  • Users may report suspected underage accounts to us for review.

4. Content Moderation

We employ the following measures to keep our platform safe:

  • User reporting: All users can report inappropriate content or accounts directly within the app.
  • Content review: Reported content is reviewed and actioned promptly. Content that violates our policies is removed.
  • User blocking: Users can block other users to prevent unwanted interactions.
  • Account moderation: We reserve the right to suspend or terminate any account that violates our community standards.

5. Reporting CSAE Content

⚠️ If you encounter any content on DiaspoAfricConnect that depicts or promotes child sexual abuse or exploitation, please report it immediately.

You can report CSAE content through the following channels:

  • In-app: Use the Report button on any content or user profile
  • Email: infos@weskillupcenter.com — include "CSAE Report" in the subject line for priority handling
  • NCMEC CyberTipline: report.cybertip.org

All reports are treated with the highest priority and confidentiality.

6. Designated Safety Contact

For any child safety concerns or inquiries related to our platform:

Email: infos@weskillupcenter.com
Subject line: Child Safety Inquiry
Response time: CSAE reports are prioritized and reviewed within 24 hours.

7. Updates to These Standards

We regularly review and update our child safety standards to ensure they remain effective and aligned with best practices. Any material changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
